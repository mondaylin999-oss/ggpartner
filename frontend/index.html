What you get

A live map, with a ghost switch

See who is actually around you right now. Share your position for 15 minutes or an hour, then it stops on its own. Ghost mode keeps you in the list and off the map.

Voice notes and voice calls

Type it, say it, or just call. Calls are peer to peer, so the audio goes straight between the two of you.

Event lobbies

Need a five-stack, a running group or four for a study table? Open a lobby with slots and let people fill it.

Verified members

Phone verification is not available in this edition. Use profiles and shared interests to find a partner.

A match score that explains itself

Every card shows how well you fit and why — same game, close rank, overlapping free time, near enough to meet.

Links to where you already are

Put your Discord, Instagram, Telegram or Facebook on your card so the conversation can carry on wherever you like.

How it works

  1. 1Sign in with Google

    Use your Google account; no separate app password is needed. Your profile, matches and chats then follow you to any device.

  2. 2Add a profile to a category

    You only appear in the categories you fill in. Add Game today and Gym next week — they are separate cards with separate details.

  3. 3Match, then talk

    Message anyone straight away, or ask to pair. Once you both say yes you can see each other's exact location on the map.

Built to hand out as little as possible

  • Distance, not your address. Your public card carries a point rounded to about a kilometre. Your real position is only ever readable by people you have matched with.
  • Ghost mode. Stay in Discover and disappear from the map, any time, with one switch.
  • Sharing expires. Live location runs on a timer you set. It stops itself.
  • Report and block. Blocking is instant and mutual — the person disappears from your list and you from theirs.
  • Your account, your profile. Sign in with Google. No payment is required.
